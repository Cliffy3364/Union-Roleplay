THE DISTRICT // FACTIONS

Find your place.Build your reputation.

The city is shaped by the groups inside it. Join a structured public service, work with a player-run business or build a criminal organisation with genuine progression and consequences. Every route should create roleplay for more than just the people inside the faction.

FACTION STANDARD Roleplay before status.

Factions are not just uniforms, ranks or access to equipment. Activity, character consistency, recruitment quality and how your group interacts with the wider city all matter.

HOW IT WORKS

Different routes. One city.

Some factions are application-based, some recruit entirely in character and others must be approved before they can operate as a recognised organisation. Check the relevant Wiki page or application route first.

01 / PUBLIC SERVICES

Structured progression

Training, ranks, specialist units and expectations around professionalism and activity.

02 / BUSINESSES

Player-led operations

Owners and managers are expected to recruit, create events and keep their business useful to the city.

03 / CRIMINAL GROUPS

Earn influence in character

Recognition should come from roleplay, relationships and consistency rather than simply claiming territory or status.
