ABOUT THE DISTRICT

Built for thepeople playing it.

The District is a 16+ British FiveM roleplay community set in Los Santos. The aim is simple: give people a city where interaction matters, characters have room to grow and the community shapes what happens next.

OUR DIRECTION

Good roleplay comes from people.

Scripts can create opportunities, but players create the moments people remember. The District is designed around giving those moments room to happen.

01 / COMMUNITY FIRST

People before systems.

The server should help people meet, create scenes and keep stories moving. Features are there to support roleplay rather than replace it.

02 / CONSEQUENCE

Stories should carry forward.

Characters, businesses, careers, relationships and rivalries should mean something beyond a single session. Choices should create new roleplay rather than end it.

03 / VARIETY

There is more than one way to play.

Public services, businesses, civilian life, crime, events and content creation all form part of the same city rather than feeling like separate game modes.

04 / EVOLUTION

The city changes with the community.

Feedback, events, new content and changing stories should keep The District moving instead of locking it into one version forever.
