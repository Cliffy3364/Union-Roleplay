ABOUT US

What we're building.

The District is designed to feel connected. What one group does should create roleplay for somebody else rather than happening in isolation.

01 / CHARACTERS

Stories that continue.

Your character should have somewhere to go. Careers, friendships, businesses, rivalries and consequences are supposed to build over time.

02 / COMMUNITY

People make the server.

Scripts support roleplay, but the actual experience comes from the people you meet and the scenes you create together.

03 / CITY LIFE

More than crime and police.

Businesses, events, civilian jobs, emergency services, content creators and everyday interactions all matter to the city.

04 / DEVELOPMENT

Always moving.

The District continues to change through community feedback, new features, events and the stories players create.
