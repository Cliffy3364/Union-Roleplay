Getting StartedKB-001

The District is a 16+ community

The minimum age to join and participate in The District is 16. We want mature, believable roleplay without turning the community into something needlessly restrictive.

01
Minimum ageYou must be at least 16 to participate in the server.
02
Act your age16+ is not permission to be toxic, disruptive or ignore Discord and server rules.
03
Roleplay firstKeep scenes believable, give other players something to work with and avoid treating the server like GTA Online.
Always read the current rulebook before playing. The wiki explains systems; the rulebook remains the authority for community and roleplay rules.

City SystemsKB-011

Getting a starter job

City Hall is used for public starter employment. Go there in character, interact with the job system and choose from the roles currently available.

Whitelisted departments and management positions are separate from normal City Hall starter jobs and may require an application or interview.

VehiclesKB-013

Buying and looking after a vehicle

PDM is the main starting point for dealership roleplay. Treat vehicles as part of your character's progression rather than disposable tools.

If your vehicle is damaged, mechanic and towing businesses create better roleplay than abandoning it and instantly replacing the situation.

Public ServicesKB-030

The District Police Service

TDPS is the server's British-inspired police service. Police roleplay is structured around response policing, command and specialist pathways.

Joining TDPS is separate from ordinary starter jobs. Follow current police recruitment or application instructions rather than expecting City Hall to assign a police role.
Public ServicesKB-031

Emergency and medical roleplay

When your character is seriously injured, treat the injury as part of the scene. Give emergency-service players time to assess and interact instead of rushing straight back into normal activity.

Emergency scenes are collaborative. The goal is believable roleplay, not simply getting healed as fast as possible.
Roleplay HelpKB-040

Building a character that lasts

A good character does not need a ten-page biography. Give them enough structure to make consistent decisions.

1
BackgroundWhere are they from and why are they in Los Santos?
2
PersonalityGive them strengths, flaws and opinions that can change through roleplay.
3
GoalsA career, business, relationships, reputation or criminal ambition creates direction.
4
ConsequencesLet failures, injuries and bad decisions affect the character instead of resetting them.
Roleplay HelpKB-041

Common roleplay terms

RDMRandom Deathmatch — attacking/killing without proper roleplay reason or escalation.
VDMVehicle Deathmatch — improperly using a vehicle as a weapon.
MetagamingUsing information your character did not legitimately learn in roleplay.
PowergamingForcing unrealistic actions or outcomes without fair interaction.
FearRPFailing to value your character's life when a believable threat exists.
NLRNew Life Rule — rules around knowledge and returning after death/respawn.
These are quick explanations only. Read the full rulebook for the exact District rule wording and enforcement.
Roleplay HelpKB-042

Criminal roleplay basics

Good criminal roleplay is about planning, tension, relationships and consequences. Crime should create scenes for both sides rather than being treated as a fastest-money route.

Do not assume an illegal mechanic exists simply because it exists on another server. Discover criminal systems through legitimate in-character roleplay.

SupportKB-051

Reporting a bug properly

The more useful the report, the faster development can reproduce and fix it.

A
What broke?Name the feature, job, app, location or action.
B
What were you doing?Write the exact steps that led to the problem.
C
Can it happen again?Say whether you can reproduce it and how often.
D
EvidenceAdd clips, screenshots or console errors if available.
