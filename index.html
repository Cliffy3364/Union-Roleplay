16+ COMMUNITY

Serious roleplay without making it miserable.

We want players who can take scenes seriously, respect other people and understand that losing, getting injured or facing consequences can create better roleplay than always winning.

01Believable characters

Your character should have goals, flaws and consistency instead of changing personality whenever it becomes convenient.

02Give and take

Good scenes are collaborative. Give other players time to react and something meaningful to work with.

03Consequences matter

Injuries, arrests, losses, damaged vehicles and failed plans are part of the story rather than problems to bypass.

04Community conduct

16+ is the minimum age, not an excuse for toxicity. Discord and in-game behaviour still have clear standards.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you ask in Discord.

The quick answers below cover the things new players normally want to know first.

How old do I need to be?

The District is a 16+ server. You need to be at least 16 to participate.

Do I need to whitelist?

Yes. Use the website Applications section after joining Discord and reading the current rules.

Where do I get a starter job?

City Hall is the starting point for available public starter jobs. Whitelisted departments and specialist roles use separate recruitment.

Where do I get my driving licence?

The temporary DMV is currently based at PDM while the dedicated driving-school setup is developed.

How do I join TDPS?

Follow current TDPS recruitment/application information. Police is not a standard City Hall job.

What if I find a bug?

Use the official bug-report route and include the steps, what you expected, what actually happened and evidence if possible.

Can I own a business?

Business opportunities depend on current availability and recruitment. Keep an eye on official business announcements and applications.

Where can I learn the server systems?

Use the new District Wiki. It is searchable and is designed to grow as more systems are added.
