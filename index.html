ABOUT THE DISTRICT

Built by roleplayers.
For roleplayers.

We are building The District around quality, immersion and stories that continue instead of resetting every time a scene ends. The city should feel connected: businesses need customers, police need genuine incidents, civilians need reasons to leave their apartments and criminals need more than a menu to press.

The minimum age is 16. Mature does not mean humourless — it means respecting scenes, other players and the fact that sometimes losing creates better roleplay than winning.

01

Quality-First Roleplay

Clear standards, believable character choices, serious scenes and an active staff team supporting the community.

02

A Thriving Community

Meet people through businesses, public services, events, Discord and the everyday life of the city.

03

Long-Term Stories

Build a reputation, career, business, relationship or criminal network and give your character somewhere to go.
